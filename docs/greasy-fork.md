HideForYou makes X (formerly Twitter) easier to browse on mobile and desktop. It can hide the **For You** tab and open **Following** first.

## What you can control

- **Timeline:** Prefer Following and hide For You.
- **Desktop sidebar:** Toggle search and recommendations separately. Search stays visible by default, while recommendations such as news, suggested accounts, and sports cards are hidden.
- **Navigation and appearance:** Hide Grok, Explore, Premium promotions, or engagement buttons. Pause filtering whenever you want.

## Install and configure

1. On **iPhone or iPad**, use Safari with the **Userscripts** extension. Open the script from the install button, then choose **Detect → Install** in Userscripts. On **desktop**, use a userscript manager such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey.
2. Open or refresh X in your browser.
3. Open **HideForYou settings** below **Settings and privacy** in X’s menu. If the menu entry is missing, use the [direct settings page](https://x.com/home#xclean-settings).

Preferences are saved in your browser. This beta is designed for the X website. Menu placement and filters may vary as X changes its interface.
